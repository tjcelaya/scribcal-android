ScribCal Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2025

Overview

ScribCal ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how ScribCal handles your data.

Data Collection

ScribCal does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

All data remains exclusively on your device and in your personal accounts:

  • Photos: Stored in your selected Google Photos album or Google Drive folder
  • Calendar Events: Written directly to your selected Google Calendar
  • App Preferences: Stored locally on your device
  • Event Types: Stored locally in the app's database on your device

Permissions

ScribCal requires the following permissions to function:

  • Camera: To capture photos for events
  • Calendar Read/Write: To write completed events to your selected calendar
  • Google Account Access: To save photos to your Google Photos or Drive
  • Internet: Required for Google API integration (Photos, Drive, Calendar)
  • Notifications: To notify you about ongoing events

Data Storage

All data is stored in one of two locations:

  1. Your Device: App settings, event types, and temporary data
  2. Your Google Account: Photos (in your chosen album/folder) and calendar events (in your selected calendar)

ScribCal has no servers, databases, or cloud storage of its own. We cannot access, view, or retrieve your data.

Data Sharing

ScribCal does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. The only data transmission occurs between your device and your own Google account services (Google Photos, Google Drive, and Google Calendar) using official Google APIs.

Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Choose which Google Calendar to use for events
  • Choose where photos are saved (Photos album or Drive folder)
  • Delete events from your calendar at any time
  • Delete photos from your Photos/Drive at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data

Children's Privacy

ScribCal does not knowingly collect any information from children or any users. The app is designed to work entirely with user-controlled Google account services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or ScribCal's data practices, please contact us through the app's GitHub repository or Google Play Store listing.
